01 · Essence

02 · Logo

The emblem and wordmark.

A diamond, the classic mark of value and a junction on a route, frames a finely serifed T. Beneath it, a small gold diamond marks the destination: a transaction safely arrived.

Primary · on light
Reversed · on navy
Stacked
Emblem
Favicon · app icon
  • Clear space. Keep space equal to the height of the emblem's T on every side.
  • Minimum size. Full logo 140px wide on screen (35mm in print); emblem 24px.
  • Backgrounds. Primary on white or ivory; reversed on navy or dark photography.
  • Wordmark. Always set in capitals with wide letter-spacing, as drawn.
  • Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to the logo.
  • Do not place the logo on busy photography without a navy veil.

Before printing, have a designer convert the wordmark text in the SVG files to outlines, so the logo looks the same on every computer and printer.

03 · Colour

Navy, ivory, and a touch of gold.

Navy carries authority; ivory and white give space and calm. Gold is a signature, not a colour scheme: use it for small accents only.

05 · Photography

Architecture, light and quiet confidence.

Choose images of strong architecture, cities and calm professional settings. Cool, natural tones. On dark sections, lay a navy veil (Midnight at 80–90%) over the photograph so text stays legible.

  • Use architecture, skylines, considered workspaces, hands on devices.
  • Avoid cash piles, padlocks, glowing circuit boards, staged handshakes.
  • People appear natural and unposed; never present stock models as staff.
  • Founder and team photos should be professional portraits on plain backgrounds.
06 · Voice

Speak as a trusted adviser.

Write the way a senior partner speaks to a bank's board: calm, specific and brief. Use British spelling consistently.
