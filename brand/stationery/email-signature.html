How to use
  1. Edit the name, title and phone below (click to type).
  2. Upload the website first, so the logo image link below works. Gmail and Outlook do not display SVG images, so export the emblem as a 88×88 px PNG (any designer or an online SVG-to-PNG converter can do this), upload it as assets/img/emblem-email.png, and change the image link below to that file.
  3. Select everything inside the white box, copy, and paste into Outlook (File › Options › Mail › Signatures) or Gmail (Settings › Signature).
