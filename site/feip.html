FEIP: every parameter, under control.

The Engineering Intelligence Platform for Oracle FLEXCUBE. Capture, approve, promote and audit every parameter change across your core banking environments.

Overview

In a core banking system, a single parameter can change how every account behaves. FEIP brings discipline, visibility and accountability to every one of those changes.

FEIP gives FLEXCUBE teams one controlled place to set up parameters, route each change through review and approval, move approved changes between environments, and keep a complete record of what changed, when, and by whom.

Capabilities

Parameterization and change control, engineered.

Parameter Capture

Set up and maintain FLEXCUBE parameters in a structured, controlled way.

Reusable Templates

Standard templates for products, branches, charges and more, applied consistently.

Maker-Checker Approvals

Every change is requested, reviewed and approved before it takes effect.

Environment Promotion

Move approved changes from development to test to production, in order.

Cross-Environment Compare

See exactly where parameters differ between environments before you promote.

Audit Trail & Rollback

A complete history of who changed what and when, with the ability to roll back.

How It Works

From request to production.

I

Capture

A parameter change is prepared, often from a template.

II

Review

A checker reviews the change and its impact.

III

Approve

Only approved changes move forward.

IV

Promote

The change moves through environments, compared at each step.

V

Audit

Every action is recorded, and any change can be rolled back.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Who is FEIP for?

Banks running Oracle FLEXCUBE, and the teams that implement, upgrade and support it, who need parameter changes to be controlled, consistent and auditable.

How does FEIP help with audits?

Every change carries a full record: who requested it, who approved it, when it was promoted to each environment, and what it changed. That history is available for internal audit and regulators.

Which FLEXCUBE versions and modules does FEIP support?

Speak with us about your FLEXCUBE version and modules. We will confirm fit during a demonstration.

Can FEIP support an implementation, upgrade or migration project?

Yes. FEIP pairs naturally with our Oracle FLEXCUBE Advisory for greenfield implementations, version upgrades and data migrations.
