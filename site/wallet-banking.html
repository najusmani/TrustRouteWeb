Your wallet, your brand.

A white-label mobile wallet platform that lets your customers onboard, pay and transfer money, under your name and on your terms.

Overview

Wallets bring new customers into the financial system. Wallet Banking gives you the platform to launch, operate and grow a wallet programme of your own.

TrustRoute Wallet Banking provides the ledger, customer journeys and back-office tools for a complete wallet programme, integrated with your core banking system, your agent network and your payment partners.

Capabilities

A complete platform, ready for your customers.

Onboarding & KYC

Digital onboarding with tiered accounts and connections to your KYC checks.

Wallet Ledger

A secure, auditable ledger for balances, limits and transaction history.

P2P Transfers

Instant transfers between wallets, by phone number or account.

Merchant Payments

QR and account-based payments at shops and online merchants.

Cash-In & Cash-Out

Through agents, branches and linked bank accounts.

Back Office & Reporting

Customer service, limits, reconciliation and regulatory reports.

How It Works

The life of a wallet.

I

Onboard

The customer registers and is verified to the right tier.

II

Fund

Money is added through an agent, branch or bank account.

III

Pay

The customer transfers, pays merchants and settles bills.

IV

Settle

Transactions are settled with your banking systems.

V

Report

Operations and compliance teams see everything in one place.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can the wallet carry our own brand?

Yes. Wallet Banking is white-label: the customer experience carries your name, colours and tone.

Do we need a licence to operate a wallet?

Wallet programmes are regulated differently in each market. We work with you, and your licensed partners where needed, to align the deployment with the requirements of your regulator.

How does the wallet connect to our core banking system?

Wallet Banking integrates with your core for funding, settlement and account linking, through standard interfaces confirmed during the assessment stage.

Can customers use the wallet without a smartphone?

Wallet services can be extended to basic phones through our SMS and USSD channel, so every customer can take part.
