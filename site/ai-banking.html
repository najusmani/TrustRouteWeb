AI Banking: intelligence at every touchpoint, under your control.

AI for customers, staff and risk teams: a customer assistant, a staff copilot, fraud and anomaly detection, and AI-assisted credit scoring and risk.

Overview

AI is most valuable in banking when it is useful, explainable and governed. AI Banking brings it to the places it helps most, with people firmly in control.

TrustRoute AI Banking connects to your core banking system and channels to help customers get answers faster, give staff instant support, spot suspicious activity earlier and inform credit decisions. Every capability is designed with oversight, audit trails and data protection from the start.

Capabilities

Four ways AI works for your bank.

Customer AI Assistant

Answers questions, checks balances and guides customers through everyday tasks on web, app or WhatsApp, handing over to a person when needed.

Staff AI Copilot

Helps staff find policies, summarise documents and draft responses, so they spend more time with customers.

Fraud & Anomaly Detection

Flags unusual transactions and behaviour for review, helping your teams act before losses grow.

Credit Scoring & Risk

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does AI make final decisions?

No. AI Banking suggests, assists and flags. Decisions on credit, fraud cases and customer outcomes remain with your people, and every action is recorded.

How is customer data protected?

Access to data is controlled and logged, and deployment options can keep data within your own environment. Data handling is agreed with your security and compliance teams before go-live.

How do you keep the assistant accurate?

The assistant answers from your approved content and data, hands questions outside its scope to your team, and its conversations can be reviewed so answers keep improving.

Can the customer assistant work on WhatsApp?

Yes. It can run alongside our WhatsApp Banking, as well as on your website and app.
