Our Story

We started TrustRoute because banks deserve advice from people who have built and run the systems they depend on, not from those selling them.

Across the banking world, institutions face the same pressures: customers who expect to bank from any phone, payment networks that change their rules, legacy switches that must keep running while new channels are added, and vendors whose interests do not always match the bank's own.

TrustRoute Technologies was founded to stand on the bank's side of that table. We combine independent advisory with a focused set of proven solutions, so our clients can move from decision to delivery with a single, accountable partner.

MissionTo give every bank technology it can trust, and the counsel to choose it well.
VisionTo be the most trusted route between banks and the customers they serve.

Leadership

Najeebullah Usmani

Founder & Principal Consultant · Former CIO & CFO

Najeebullah Usmani founded TrustRoute Technologies to bring practitioner-led, independent counsel to banks and financial institutions. His work spans core banking delivery, digital channel strategy, payment switching, and the testing and certification of payment systems.

Before founding TrustRoute, Najeebullah built 20 years of experience across the Middle East, Asia, the Caribbean and North America, serving as Chief Information Officer and as Chief Financial Officer at different financial and consultancy institutions, and leading the delivery of core banking systems. Having led both technology and finance functions, he brings a rare perspective to every engagement: an understanding of what a system must do, and of what it must be worth.
