Privacy Notice

Draft for review. This notice is a starting template and must be reviewed by legal counsel for the jurisdictions in which TrustRoute Technologies operates before the site goes live.

TrustRoute Technologies ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, institution, role, email address, phone number, country and message.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry and, where you have asked us to, to keep you informed about our services and insights.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate this website and our communications, under appropriate safeguards. Enquiries are stored in our database hosted by Supabase and delivered to our inbox by Resend, our email provider. If you book a call, your booking is handled by Calendly under its own privacy policy.

Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our correspondence.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. To make a request, write to hello@trustroutetechnologies.com.
