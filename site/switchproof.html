Questions

Frequently asked.

Is SwitchProof only for Oracle FLEXCUBE?

SwitchProof is designed for banks running Oracle FLEXCUBE. If you run a different core banking system, speak with us about your requirements.

What is stand-in processing?

When the core banking system or host is unavailable, stand-in processing lets transactions continue to be authorised within limits you define. They are then posted and reconciled when the system is back, so customers are not turned away during an outage.

Does SwitchProof replace scheme certification?

No. Formal certification is carried out with the scheme or network. SwitchProof helps you prepare for it, by finding and fixing issues early and keeping clear evidence of your testing.

Can SwitchProof work alongside FEIP and your advisory services?

Yes. FEIP controls how FLEXCUBE parameter changes are made and approved; SwitchProof proves those changes work. Both pair with our Oracle FLEXCUBE Advisory for implementations, upgrades and migrations.
