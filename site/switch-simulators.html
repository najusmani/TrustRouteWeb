Overview

Real terminals and live networks are slow and costly to test against. Our simulators let your teams reproduce any scenario, on demand.

TrustRoute Switch Test Simulators stand in for every party in a card or account transaction. Teams build scenarios once, run them as often as needed, and keep the evidence for audit and certification preparation.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Which message formats are supported?

The simulators are built around ISO 8583-based message flows and are configured to your switch's specification. Speak with us about the formats and variants you need.

Can the simulators run inside our own test environment?

Yes. They can be installed in your test lab, so no test data leaves your environment.

Do the simulators replace scheme certification?

No. Formal certification is carried out with the scheme or network. The simulators help you prepare for it, by finding and fixing issues early and keeping clear evidence of your testing.

Can we combine the simulators with your testing advisory?

Yes. Many clients pair the simulators with our Payments Testing & Certification advisory for a complete test strategy.
